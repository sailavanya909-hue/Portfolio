Limits of this case study

The data represents a fictional business. Recommendations require review by planning, procurement, finance, and quality stakeholders. Quality release is not assumed for quarantined lots. The model does not authorize material use or replace validated planning or quality processes.

Cost exposure is an estimate under the stated demand and expiry assumptions. A real business outcome would require an implemented action and evidence of its effect.
